Go Green India 🌿

What is Go Green?

Go Green means taking care of our environment. It means planting more trees, saving water, stopping pollution and keeping our surroundings clean. India is a beautiful country with many forests, rivers and mountains. We must protect them for our future.

Every small step counts. If every student in India plants one tree, crores of trees will be planted. That is the power of teamwork and responsibility.

Why Should We Save Trees?

Trees give us oxygen to breathe. Without trees, we cannot survive. Trees also give us fruits, wood, shade and medicine. They are home to many birds and animals.

When we cut trees, it causes floods, droughts and global warming. So we must stop cutting trees and start planting new ones every day.

How Can Students Help?

As students, we can do many things to help our environment. Here are some easy ways :

- Plant a tree on your birthday.
- Do not waste water.
- Use both sides of paper.
- Do not throw garbage on roads.
- Say no to plastic bags.
- Walk or cycle instead of using vehicles.
- Switch off lights when not needed.

Save Water, Save Life

Water is very important for all living beings. India is facing a water shortage problem in many states. We must use water carefully and not waste even a single drop.

Close the tap while brushing teeth. Use bucket instead of shower. Collect rainwater and use it for plants. These small habits can save thousands of liters of water every year.

Stop Pollution

Air pollution, water pollution and land pollution are big problems in India. Smoke from factories and vehicles makes the air dirty. Garbage thrown in rivers makes water dirty.

We can stop pollution by using less petrol and diesel, by not burning garbage, by not throwing waste in rivers and by using eco friendly products in our daily life.
